Terms and Privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

FindIt is a lost-and-found service for the USTED community. Please read this page. By creating an account you agree to it.

1. Using FindIt

  • Give true information. Do not post items that do not exist.
  • Do not claim an item that is not yours.
  • Do not post anything illegal, abusive, or private about other people.
  • Keep your password and your SMS codes to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One person, one account.

2. What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and, if you give it, your email.
  • Your password. We keep it in scrambled form and cannot read it.
  • What you post: item details, photos, claim messages and reports.
  • The time you accepted these Terms.
  • A cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 minutes.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone can see an open post: its title, category, place, date, description and photos. Your name and phone number are not shown on your posts.
  • The private detail on a found item (the thing only the real owner would know) is never shown to the public. Only you, as the person who posted it, can see it.
  • Your phone number is shared with the other person only after the owner approves a claim. The owner's number is shared only if the owner allowed contact on that post.
  • Admins can see the name, phone number, email and role of every account. Moderators can see reported posts and the reasons given.

4. Text messages

We send SMS messages for sign-up codes, password resets, claims and moderation decisions. They are sent through Arkesel, so your phone number and the message go through their service.

5. Photos

When you upload a photo, we save a clean copy without hidden data such as the place it was taken or the camera used.

6. Rules and removal

  • Any member can report a post. Moderators can remove a post that breaks these rules.
  • An account that breaks the rules can be suspended. You will get an SMS, and you will not be able to log in until it is restored.
  • An open post disappears from search after 60 days.

7. Meeting safely

FindIt helps people get in touch. It does not check people or items, and it is not responsible for what happens when you meet. Meet in a public place on campus, and do not hand over an item until you are sure it belongs to the person asking.

8. Your data and your rights

Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) gives you rights over your personal data. To ask what we hold about you, or to ask for your account to be removed, contact the FindIt team: osmanalhassaniddrisu50@gmail.com. Deleting an account is not yet possible on the site, so we handle requests by hand.

9. Changes

We may change this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
